Overview

Built for chemical manufacturing

Batch production and effective traceability,
finally in one place.

Gravivity replaces spreadsheet chaos with real-time stock visibility, role-based access, and full production traceability – from raw material to finished batch.

  • See it in action
What it does

One system for the whole production floor

From receiving stock to signing off a finished batch

Real-Time Inventory

Live stock levels, low-stock alerts, and barcode-based receiving keep the floor and the office in sync.

Batch Traceability

Every batch tracks its materials, cost, specific gravity, and full production timeline – planned to completed.

Role-Based Access

Production staff, inventory managers, sales, and admins each see exactly what their role needs – nothing more.

Automated Calculations

Specific gravity, batch cost, and material usage are computed automatically, not tallied by hand.
